PhotoBot Privacy Policy

PhotoBot is a personal app used only by its owner. It receives pictures that the owner sends by text message and uploads them to the owner's own Google Photos account.

What the app accesses: the app can only add new pictures to the owner's Google Photos. It cannot view, change, or delete anything else in the account.

What the app stores: the app does not keep copies of the pictures. They are uploaded to Google Photos and then discarded. The app stores only the sign-in token that Google provides, so it can keep uploading.

Sharing: the app does not sell, share, or give any data to anyone else.
